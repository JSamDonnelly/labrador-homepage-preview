VPATs & ACRs

These are documents, not audits. A VPAT (the Voluntary Product Accessibility Template) filled out becomes an ACR, an Accessibility Conformance Report: the proof procurement asks for. Every VPAT is an add-on to an audit, because we can only report on what we've tested; if you haven't been audited yet, that's where we start. Each edition below is priced as the document alone, and the full story lives on the VPATs page.

WCAG edition
$750
The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines alone: the edition for buyers who simply want to know the product is accessible.
508 edition
$950
Adds Section 508, for United States federal procurement.
EU edition
$950
Adds EN 301 549, for public procurement across the European Union.
INT edition
$1,500
All three standards in one document, for products sold into more than one market.
