We built the software our audits run on.

Labrador is our guided WCAG conformance testing platform: the reason our audits arrive consistent, evidenced, and genuinely readable. We built it for our own needs first. Today it's also a product other consultancies use, and every engagement you buy from us gets the full benefit of it.

The export your team actually reads

Every audit ships as an interactive HTML report generated straight from the tested record. Filter findings by page, severity, or criterion; follow any finding to the exact success criterion it fails; see every claim sitting beside its evidence. One self-contained file your whole team can open.

The sample is a complete, real audit of University of Montauk, a demonstration site we built so we could audit it in the open. Every filter, link, and piece of evidence in the download works exactly as it does in the reports our clients receive.

Developers actually enjoy working with these audit reports rather than scrolling spreadsheets. Our favorite review so far: an agency developer who, after one audit with our most recent format, asked whether the other auditors they work with could switch to Labrador too.

What it changes about your audit

Labrador is why the quality of our work survives contact with deadlines. Four things it locks in:

  • Guided, criterion by criterion

    Testers work every success criterion in order, page by page and component by component, with the criterion's requirements in view as they test. Nothing gets skipped because someone was tired on a Thursday.

  • Evidence lives with the finding

    Screenshots and video attach to the finding they document. The report is generated from that record, never retyped into it, so nothing is lost between the testing and the delivery.

  • An export for every audience

    Reviewers get the interactive HTML report. Procurement gets a DOCX VPAT. Developers get both routes into Jira: a Jira-ready CSV export, and a direct Jira integration that moves findings into the backlog with no file in between. And when someone just wants a spreadsheet, a plain CSV opens clean in Excel or Google Sheets.

  • Remediation, tracked to the end

    Every finding carries a remediation status through to the re-test, so "are we done yet?" always has a current, honest answer.

Where it stops

It structures manual testing; it replaces nothing.

Labrador never runs on your site. It is not a widget, not an overlay, and nothing about it touches your code: it is the workbench our auditors test at, and the exports are what leave it.

The judgment stays human. The platform decides nothing about your conformance; it makes sure the people who do decide are looking at everything, in order, with the evidence in reach.
